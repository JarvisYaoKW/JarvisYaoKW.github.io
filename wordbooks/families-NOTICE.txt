Bundled word-family supplement (20261029-1)

Lexical relationships and English senses: Princeton WordNet 3.0.
Source distribution: https://github.com/nltk/nltk_data/blob/gh-pages/packages/corpora/wordnet.zip
Documentation: https://wordnet.princeton.edu/documentation/wninput5wn
License: WordNet-LICENSE.txt (preserved in full).

Only explicit lexical derivationally related forms (+) and adverb-to-adjective
derived-from pointers are used. Edges are made available in both directions.
Synonyms, antonyms, broad semantic relations and guessed suffix forms are excluded.
Source POS must match the learner's currently imported senses. Target Chinese
senses must match the target POS. BNC/FRQ ranks <= 50000, Collins/Oxford entries
and existing preset members are retained. At most 12 links are kept per root.

Chinese meanings and primary IPA: ECDICT (MIT), source and SHA256 in families-lock.json.
Supplementary missing US IPA: open-dict-data/ipa-dict en_US (MIT), based on
cmudict-ipa and syllabify as credited by that project. Only single-reading
matches are used to avoid assigning an arbitrary homograph pronunciation.
Source: https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict/blob/master/data/en_US.txt
Full license: IPA-DICT-LICENSE.txt. This release does not bundle en_UK data.
Missing IPA remains explicitly missing and is looked up by the existing practice UI.

The supplement is a read-only resource, separate from personal entries, scores,
favorites, coins and queues. Manual family definitions take precedence. Each
target has its own POS-specific Chinese and English definition; a relation is
not a claim that a suffix preserves every sense of the source word.

Coverage, source hashes and generation rules: families-lock.json.
Reproducible generator: tools/build-families.py (Python standard library only).
