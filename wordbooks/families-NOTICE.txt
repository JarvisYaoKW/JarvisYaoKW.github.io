Complete word-family supplement (20261104-2)

Relations and English definitions: Princeton WordNet 3.0 (WordNet-LICENSE.txt).
Chinese definitions and primary IPA: ECDICT, pinned inputs in families-lock.json.
Supplementary single-reading US IPA: ipa-dict en_US (IPA-DICT-LICENSE.txt).

The generator traverses explicit lexical sense nodes, never jumping between
synonyms or between unconnected senses of a spelling. Multi-step paths are
preserved. Derivational pointers require compatible morphology; irregular
stem pairs are explicitly listed in tools/family_graph.py. Prefix counterparts
require both an explicit WordNet antonym pointer and a matching prefix. Relational
adjectives require an explicit pertainym link plus matching morphology.
Three editorial relationship bridges reference Wiktionary pages for act/active,
active/activate and create/creator. Their references are retained in links.
Creator's corrupted source Chinese is repaired with an explicitly labelled
editorial meaning. No Wiktionary definition prose is reproduced.

Every intermediate and target needs a POS-matched Chinese definition. Archaic
or corrupted Chinese records are excluded. Common/extended frequency metadata
is retained; there is no fixed member-count cap. Coverage is not exhaustive.

families.json is the complete audit dataset. The browser requests only the
current initial from families/a.json ... families/z.json, caches each pack,
and uses existing cached packs offline. A never-downloaded pack needs a network
connection to the site. Vocabulary relations themselves are prebuilt; they do
not require a live external dictionary search. Audio and missing IPA may still
use the existing pronunciation services.

This is a read-only dictionary overlay. Personal vocabulary and progress are
not replaced; manual meanings take precedence. Details: families-lock.json;
rebuild: tools/build-families.py and tools/family_graph.py.
