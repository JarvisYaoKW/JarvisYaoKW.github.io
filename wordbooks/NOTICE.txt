Exam tags and translations are reproduced from the pinned CSV. The IELTS core subset is ranked by generic corpus frequency, not exam frequency.
The ECDICT books are not official Shanbay books. The added IELTS Vocabulary Scripture wordlist is user-requested and documented separately in scripture-source-notice.txt. No user progress, favorites, original PDF scans, generated examples, or invented word forms are bundled.

English definitions: pinned ECDICT definition field (MIT). Small gaps have independently worded editorial glosses, explicitly labelled in each entry. Form-only notes describe the source-provided inflection and link to the lemma. Coverage and hashes: english-definitions-lock.json.
