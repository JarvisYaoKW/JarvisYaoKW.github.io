Exam tags and translations are reproduced from the pinned CSV. The IELTS core subset is ranked by generic corpus frequency, not exam frequency.
The ECDICT books are not official Shanbay books. No user progress, favorites, original PDF scans, generated examples, or invented word forms are bundled.

English definitions: pinned ECDICT definition field (MIT). Small gaps have independently worded editorial glosses, explicitly labelled in each entry. Form-only notes describe the source-provided inflection and link to the lemma. Coverage and hashes: english-definitions-lock.json.

Word-family supplement: WordNet 3.0 lexical derivations and adverb/adjective links,
with POS-matched ECDICT Chinese meanings and IPA. Details, source versions,
additional IPA attribution and licenses: families-NOTICE.txt and families-lock.json.

Offline verb inflections (verb-inflections.mjs): p/d/i/3 exchange fields from the
same pinned ECDICT CSV (MIT). Missing fields are supplemented from UniMorph English,
adapted by selecting verbal inflections, matching ECDICT lemmas and merging slots.
UniMorph English contributors, source: https://github.com/unimorph/eng/tree/66e0e9e8e2dcd196da081a25a48e5c1fe3d8b49b
UniMorph-derived data is offered under CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
tools/build-verb-inflections.py also records common spelling variants checked
against Cambridge Dictionary (https://dictionary.cambridge.org/).
